About AcuraHondaSpecs

AcuraHondaSpecs was created to simplify the process of working on Acura and Honda motors. After over 10 years of hands-on experience building everything from factory motors to fully built, sleeved blocks ready for 1200hp, we’ve seen how easily torque specs, sequences, and other important details can get lost or mixed up over time. After countless hours searching multiple websites to find the correct specifications, we decided to centralize everything in one place.

Our goal is to provide a comprehensive, easy-to-navigate resource where you can find torque specs, sequences, and other crucial information for your Acura and Honda projects. Whether you’re a DIY enthusiast working on a stock engine or a professional building a high-performance machine, we aim to make your job easier and more efficient. We also offer downloadable torque sequence charts for quick reference, so you don’t have to hunt down the information again.

We plan to continually update and expand our database, ensuring that every specification you need is available at your fingertips. If there’s something specific you’re looking for, feel free to reach out and let us know. We are always looking for ways to improve.

When finding the specs for our website, we used OEM Maintenance Manual's, NTHEFASTLANE and Honda to ensure we found the most accurate data!

Our Mission

At AcuraHondaSpecs, our mission is simple: to make working on Honda and Acura motors as straightforward and efficient as possible. We strive to provide a one-stop resource for the torque specs, sequences, and technical information that every builder and enthusiast needs, all in one place. We understand the frustration of searching through multiple sources to find the right specs, which is why we are committed to offering accurate, reliable, and up-to-date information for every type of engine, whether it's a daily driver or a high-performance machine pushing 1,000+ horsepower.

Our mission is not just to provide data; it's to empower our users by making essential information easily accessible, organized, and downloadable for convenience. We are dedicated to continually updating our database with new specs and sequences as technology evolves, ensuring that our community stays ahead of the curve.

We’re driven by the passion for Honda and Acura performance, and our goal is to help enthusiasts and professionals alike build, maintain, and modify their motors with confidence. Whether you're a first-time DIYer or a seasoned professional, we are here to support you every step of the way.

How We Collect Data

All torque specs are gathered from verified OEM service manuals and trusted technical resources. Whenever possible, we cross-reference values to ensure accuracy. Our aim is to provide specs you can trust, every time.

Looking Ahead

Currently, we only B-Series engines. We are expanding our database to include K, J, D, and other Honda engine families. If you’d like to contribute or request a specific engine, please contact us.
